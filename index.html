05 / THE RESPONSE

Fast decisions.
Human control.

Industrial security must consider operational safety. Sentinel demonstrates containment playbooks, asset priorities, and operator confirmation.

Detect→Assess→Confirm

Response actions in this portfolio are simulated.

The local copilot adds retrieval-based guidance and a voice interface to the investigation workflow.

06 / THE EVIDENCE

The incident ends.
The record remains.

From an anomaly to its explanation and proposed response, Sentinel connects the investigation into an audit trail.

Control mappings help relate the response to security governance. Exportable records support a later review.
