# Sentinel-IoT watchful eye

The custom eye represents observation, detection, and a clear view of an alert.
Its mint upper contour and ivory lower contour surround a circular iris and
coral diamond pupil. Two small circuit terminals connect it to industrial IoT.
It uses the website's existing colors and remains a crisp, editable vector.

`public/brand/sentinel-eye.svg` is the transparent master asset. Vite embeds its
mark once into the initial page response; the header, footer, splash center,
and splash signature reuse that symbol. There is no extra logo request before
the loading screen paints. The splash still opens automatically within two
seconds. Decorative copies remain hidden from assistive technology, while the
home links keep their accessible names.

`public/brand/sentinel-eye-icon.svg` is a simplified favicon with a charcoal
background for contrast in both light and dark browser chrome. It omits the
circuit details at tiny sizes. The previous favicon file remains available.

Keep the mark's 3:2 proportions. The header uses 42 × 28 px on desktop and a
smaller visible mark on phones. Use a dark plain background for the mint and
ivory master. The logo is static in the header; replay uses one short, uniform
reveal that respects reduced motion and the existing pause setting.
